Our Story

Art Born from
Passion & Tradition

Saraswati Arts is a small studio rooted in Chondhi, Alibag, creating bespoke decorative pieces that celebrate life's most precious moments — from wedding anniversaries to birthday milestones and festive occasions.

Each piece is crafted by hand with premium materials, bringing together traditional artistry and contemporary design to give you something truly one-of-a-kind.
